What it does

Everything you need in 4 cards.

Easy registration

Name, blood group, city & phone. One submit and you're in the directory.

Smart search

Filter by blood group + neighbourhood to find nearby donors instantly.

Emergency alerts

Critical needs pop in bold red so donors act without scrolling.

How it works

Register → Search → Contact.

That's the whole flow. No accounts, no backend — data stays in your browser.

01

Register yourself

Fill a 30-second form. Your card shows up in the directory right away.

02

Search & filter

Pick a blood group, type an area. Matches update as you type.

03

Call the donor

Tap the number to contact a voluntary donor directly.
